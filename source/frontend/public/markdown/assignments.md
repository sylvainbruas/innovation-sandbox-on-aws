---
title: Assignments
---

Manage who has access to this lease. The Assignments tab shows users and groups currently assigned, and lets you add or remove access.

---

## Staged changes

Changes you make (add or remove) are staged locally until you choose **Save changes**. Navigate away without saving to discard.

---

## Access types

**Direct**: Individual user access granted by email address.

**Group**: Access granted via an Identity Center group. All group members gain access automatically.

---

## Processing states

After saving, assignments show a processing indicator while changes are applied. If an assignment fails, save changes again to retry.

---

## Who can manage assignments

**Admins and Managers** can always add or remove assignments.

**Lease Owners** can manage assignments when the **Allow owner to share lease** setting is enabled on the lease.

_Note: This tab is visible to the lease owner, Admins, and Managers on Active leases._

For more information, see [Sharing leases](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/solutions/latest/innovation-sandbox-on-aws/manager-guide.html#sharing-leases).
