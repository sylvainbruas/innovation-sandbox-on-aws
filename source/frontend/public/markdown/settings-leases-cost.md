Each group of settings has its own **Save** button, so you can update one group at a time. Admins can edit settings; managers can only view them. Tabs with never-saved settings show a badge with the count, so you can find and complete initial setup.

### [Lease Policies](/settings#lease-policies)

Maximum values for budget, lease duration, and leases per user, plus lease request rate limits and self-termination behavior. Individual lease templates created by managers cannot have a budget or lease duration that exceeds these global settings. For example, if the Admin sets **Max Budget** to $500, then a lease template created by managers cannot have a budget higher than $500.

This section also includes **Enable lease sharing** (controls whether owners can share leases) and **Enable principal search** (controls whether the typeahead search is available when adding assignments).

### [Cost Reporting](/settings#cost-reporting)

The cost report groups and whether selecting one is required when requesting a lease.
