---
title: Shared Leases
---

View sandbox accounts that have been shared with you by lease owners, Admins, or Managers.

---

## Access types

**Direct**: You were granted access individually by email address.

**Group**: You have access because you belong to an Identity Center group that was assigned to the lease.

---

## Discovering shared leases

Shared leases appear automatically in your **Active Leases** section and on the Leases page in the **Shared with me** tab. No action is required to accept access.

---

## Group membership

When access is granted via a group, membership changes in IAM Identity Center are reflected automatically. If you are added to or removed from a group, your access updates accordingly.

For more information, see [Shared leases](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/solutions/latest/innovation-sandbox-on-aws/user-section.html#shared-leases).
